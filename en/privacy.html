Privacy policy

HyFusion · updated November 2, 2026

HyFusion doesn’t collect personal data. The app has no account, server, ads, or analytics or tracking tools. Everything you create stays on your iPhone and your Apple Watch.

Workouts

The workouts you build or import are stored on your iPhone and sent to your Apple Watch over the connection between the two devices.

Importing photos and PDFs

When you import a photo, a camera image or a PDF, the text is read on your iPhone itself. The image and the text never leave the device, and the received file is deleted after it’s read.

Health app

With your permission, Apple Watch saves workouts to the Health app, with heart rate, calories, distance and the run route. The iPhone reads these workouts to show your history. This data stays in the Health app, under your control, and is never sent to HyFusion or third parties. You can revoke access at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

Location

During run blocks, Apple Watch uses your location to measure distance and pace. It’s only used while the workout is in progress and is saved only in the Health app.
