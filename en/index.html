Support

Found a problem or have a suggestion? Open an issue at github.com/fernandoblueintra/hyfusion-site/issues. Tell us your iPhone and watch models and what happened.

Common questions

The workout doesn’t show up on the watch. Open HyFusion on your iPhone and check the status line in the workout list. The watch must be paired and have HyFusion installed.

The history is empty. Allow HyFusion to read workouts in the Health app, in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > HyFusion.

Import can’t read the photo. The text is read right on your iPhone. Use a straight, well-lit photo of the board, or crop it to the workout.
